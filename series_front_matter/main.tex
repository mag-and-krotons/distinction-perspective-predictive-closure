\section*{Release declaration}
All manuscripts use the same A4 academic style and name Abhijit Singh as sole author. The individual packages are the submission objects; the atlas and combined volume are navigational and archival objects. Raw handwritten source notes are excluded from every academic manuscript and submission asset. Exact, finite, empirical, conditional, conjectural, and open claims retain their stated status.
