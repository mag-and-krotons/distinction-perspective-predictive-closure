# Distinction, Perspective, and Predictive Closure — Research Release 8.0.2

In this release, I publish the complete current form of my research programme:
eighteen specialist papers, one scientific atlas, a two-hundred-page combined
series, individual submission packages, data, figures, source files, executable
audits, and provenance ledgers.

## Scientific scope

Release 8.0.2 integrates:

- the occurrence-sensitive prime triad;
- the nine-state / thirty-six-relation prime-observer closure theorem;
- transparent-prime sector labels and their scoped quantum-number
  interpretation;
- the repdigit terminal-lattice realization;
- the corrected all-prime Möbius predictive quotient; and
- the connections of these constructions to the wider programme on recursive
  distinction, representation genesis, predictive closure, quantum
  instruments, and recurrence.

I preserve the status of every claim. Definitions, results proved within the
formalism, representations, numerical checks, physical hypotheses, and open
questions are identified as such; computational evidence is not presented as
experimental confirmation or as a proof of an unresolved classical theorem.
