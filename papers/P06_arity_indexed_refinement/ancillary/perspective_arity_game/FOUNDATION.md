# Corrected perspective-arity foundation

## Semantic index

- \(P_0\): no distinction. This is TRUE \(R\). It has no program state,
  resultants, objects, laws, number, language, or mathematics.
- \(P_1\): the perspective native to us. Its primitive distinction has two
  co-resultants. Every statement, equation and program in this branch is made
  from \(P_1\).
- \(P_q\): a hypothetical perspective whose one primitive distinction has
  \(q\) co-resultants. The symbol \(q\) is only a \(P_1\) coordinate for what
  \(P_1\) cannot experience natively.

In particular, \(P_4\) is not four binary-labelled states and is not two
successive \(P_1\) distinctions. Its four-resultant relation is atomic.

## Sole source rule

\[
\text{distinction}\longmapsto\text{all of its co-resultants}.
\]

The resultants are not things that existed before being compared. They are
co-created terms of one relation. No resultant is preferred and none exists
alone.

The primitive may be present more than once. Because the sole rule does not
decide how occurrences share resultants, the source closure retains every
finite sharing arrangement. Selecting a spatial neighbourhood, update order,
survival condition, cost, probability, arithmetic, information variable,
knowledge variable, or target pattern would introduce a \(P_1\) law.

## What the game does

The program cannot enter \(P_4\). It uses \(P_1\) to:

1. present one atomic four-resultant occurrence as one incidence;
2. generate every finite continuation without choosing one;
3. erase names and construction order when auditing native structure;
4. retain all raw generating histories behind every audit class;
5. enumerate every complete binary hierarchy \(P_1\) can impose on each
   four-resultant occurrence; and
6. keep those binary hierarchies as a fiber over the same native \(P_4\)
   presentation.

There are fifteen complete non-plane binary trees over four labelled
resultants: three balanced and twelve unbalanced. All fifteen are retained.
Restricting to equal-depth two-bit codes is recorded only as a named subview.

The complete binary fibers are effects of \(P_1\)'s limit. They are not
\(P_4\)'s mathematics.

## Non-elimination

No branch is deleted because:

- \(P_1\) cannot interpret it;
- two histories look isomorphic in one audit;
- one binary encoding is convenient;
- it fails to resemble a desired \(P_1\) phenomenon; or
- it does not help RH.

Classification forms a reversible fiber map. It never replaces the source.

## Admissible conclusions

A native conclusion must remain unchanged under every renaming of resultants,
every renaming of atomic events, every implementation order, and every faithful
\(P_1\) token encoding.

Statements about counts, binary pairings, files, loops, or event horizons are
labelled \(P_1\) audits unless representation invariance is proved.
