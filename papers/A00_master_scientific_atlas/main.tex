\section{Evaluation of the research programme}
The corpus is not one unsupported hypothesis. It contains four different scientific objects that must be judged separately:
\begin{enumerate}
\item exact mathematical theorems with finite declared assumptions;
\item executable constructions and reproducible numerical calculations;
\item empirical results or audits tied to specified datasets;
\item physical models and conjectural bridges with explicit falsification conditions.
\end{enumerate}
Calling the whole body ``a hypothesis'' erases the theorem and computation layers. Calling every branch ``proved'' would erase the remaining experimental and universal obligations. The accurate verdict is stronger and more discriminating: this is a genuine multidisciplinary research programme with original organizing principles, many exact internal results, several high-value empirical and physical tests, and a smaller set of open bridges whose failure would narrow rather than nullify the proved core.
