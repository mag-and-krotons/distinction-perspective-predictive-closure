# Academic-source separation

Release 8.0.1 corrected a publication error in its predecessor build. Release
8.0.2 preserves that separation while repairing integrity and publication
metadata.

Raw handwritten research notes are provenance records, not academic figures. They are therefore excluded from:

- every manuscript PDF;
- every paper source package;
- every individual submission package;
- the combined paper series;
- public Git history and release assets;
- Zenodo-ready public upload assets.

P17 now contains only typeset definitions, theorems, proofs, tables, formal diagrams, data, and executable verification. The handwritten notes are preserved separately in a private provenance archive outside the public academic release.
