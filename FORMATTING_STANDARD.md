# Uniform Academic Formatting Standard - Suite Version 7.0

This edition follows one portfolio-wide standard modelled on the approved *Distinction-Generated Perspectives* layout.

- **Page:** A4 throughout.
- **Body:** 11-point New TX serif text with matching mathematical fonts.
- **Headings:** Helvetica-family sans serif; one maroon accent and one neutral gray system.
- **Margins:** 22 mm left/right, 21 mm top, 22 mm bottom.
- **Title page:** fixed masthead, paper ID, version, date, title, subtitle, and author in identical positions.
- **Authorship:** Abhijit Singh only; no affiliation line.
- **Front matter:** title page, contents page, abstract page, keywords, scope/status, citation, then body.
- **Running matter:** short title and paper ID in the header; centered page number in the footer.
- **Numbering:** one automatic section-numbering system. Manually embedded section numbers are removed.
- **Theorems and definitions:** one maroon-labelled theorem family; source-style statement labels are normalized.
- **Figures and tables:** common caption typography, spacing, table density, and float separation.
- **References:** common heading and reduced-size bibliography block.
- **PDF metadata:** full title and sole author embedded in every PDF.
- **Quality control:** clean compilation, A4 audit, warning audit, full rendering, and visual contact-sheet inspection.
