# Distinction, Perspective, and Predictive Closure

**Abhijit Singh**
**Complete Research Release 8.0.2 — 27 August 2026**

I present a cumulative research programme on distinction, perspective,
predictive closure, representation genesis, quantum instruments, prime arity,
and recurrence. This release brings together eighteen specialist papers and one
scientific atlas as a two-hundred-page research series, with the data, figures,
formal derivations, executable audits, submission packages, and provenance
ledgers needed to examine and reproduce the work.

The programme develops the occurrence-sensitive prime triad, the nine-state /
thirty-six-relation prime-observer closure theorem, transparent-prime sector
labels, a scoped quantum-number interpretation, the repdigit terminal lattice,
and the corrected all-prime Möbius predictive quotient. It also records the
limits of each result: definitions, results proved within the formalism,
representations, numerical checks, physical hypotheses, and open questions are
kept explicitly distinct.
